Questions brands ask before a first campaign

Audience, verification, industries and how to begin.

Who is the Funngro audience?

70 Lakh+ young Indians aged 14–25. Every account is verified by OTP at sign-up and checked for duplicates, so one person means one account.

How is Funngro different from ads or influencer agencies?

Ad platforms sell impressions and agencies sell reach. Funngro delivers verified actions from an opted-in audience: promoting a product, trying it, referring a friend or giving feedback.

How are actions verified?

Users complete the task as briefed and upload proof, such as a photo, a screenshot or a link. You verify the work before the payout is released.

Which industries does Funngro work with?

Fintech, D2C, beauty, FMCG, gaming, edtech, food and beverage, commerce, travel, entertainment, wellness and lifestyle.

Email hello@funngro.com with a short note about your brand, your audience and what you want to test. The team replies with a suggested cohort, work type and starting point.

Is user data handled compliantly?

Yes. Funngro's data handling is DPDP-compliant, data stays in India and tracking is consent-first.
