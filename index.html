How to start earning online in 5 steps

All you need is a smartphone or laptop with internet, a UPI ID, and about two minutes to sign up.

  1. Download the Funngro app

    Get Funngro from Google Play or the App Store and sign up with an OTP. It is free, and there is nothing to pay at any point.

  2. Pick a brand campaign

    Browse live campaigns from 5,000+ brands and choose the work that suits you: promotions, sampling, referrals, surveys, content or influencer briefs.

  3. Complete the task

    Every campaign comes with clear instructions. Do the task, then upload your proof as a photo, a screenshot or a link.

  4. Get paid in UPI

    Once the brand verifies your work, the money goes to your UPI. Your first payout arrives within 24 hours, repeat payouts land in seconds, and there are no withdrawal fees.

  5. Level up and earn more

    Move from Starter to Grower to Builder. Higher tiers unlock higher-paying campaigns, and you can lead your own team as a Clan Leader.
